# IHIS — Imam Hussein Islamic Society

The IHIS app for Android: Edmonton prayer times, the Islamic and Persian calendars, community events, and the athan.

## Download

### [⬇ Download IHIS for Android](https://github.com/mahdif355/IHIS-app/releases/latest/download/IHIS.apk)

Tap that link on your phone, then open the downloaded file to install it.

The link always gives the newest version, so it is safe to share and to keep.

If Android warns about installing from unknown sources, allow it for this once. Installing over an existing copy keeps all your settings.

Older versions, and what each one changed, are on the [Releases](../../releases) page.

### After installing

- Allow **notifications** when asked, or the athan cannot show on screen.
- On **Android 14 or newer**, allow **Alarms & reminders** when asked. Without it, Android can delay the athan by up to half an hour.
- If you also have another prayer app with its athan turned on, such as Persian Calendar or Badesaba, you will hear two athans. Turn the athan off in one of them.
